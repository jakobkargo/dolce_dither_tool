dolce_dither_tool
=================

Run it
  Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser and works offline:
  nothing to install, no server needed, and your images never leave
  your computer.

Files
  index.html   the page
  style.css    layout and colours (light and dark mode)
  app.js       the settings, live preview, dragging and download
  engine.js    the dithering itself

Changing things
  Default settings:      DEFAULTS near the top of app.js
  Palette presets:       PRESETS near the top of app.js
  Slider ranges/labels:  SECTIONS in app.js
  Colours and fonts:     the variables at the top of style.css
